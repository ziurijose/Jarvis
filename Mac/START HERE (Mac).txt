JARVIS - voice assistant for your Mac
=====================================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example, Documents).

2. Double-click "Start JARVIS.command".
   The first time, a Terminal window installs everything JARVIS needs, into this folder:
   its speech recognition and natural voice, the free AI model that runs on your Mac (the
   fastest one your Mac runs well, with TurboLLM to run it), and Claude Code for the hardest
   requests. That's several GB, so it takes a while and needs internet, but no password.

   Near the end it opens two Mac settings, one at a time: switch Terminal on in each
   (Accessibility: so JARVIS can use the mouse, keyboard and hotkey; Screen Recording: so
   it can see the screen). If Terminal isn't in the list, click + and add it from
   Applications > Utilities.

   If your Mac says it can't verify or open the file:
     - Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway".
     - Or right-click the file and choose Open.
     - Or open Terminal, type   bash   and a space, drag the file into the window,
       and press Return.

3. A setup window appears. Type your name, pick a name for your assistant, then let it
   scan your face. That scan is the face lock: JARVIS starts only for you.

4. When macOS asks, allow Terminal to use the Camera and Microphone.
   (Missed it? System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera / Microphone.)

5. Optional: JARVIS answers with the free model on your Mac and hands the hardest requests
   to Claude Code. To let it, sign in to Claude Code once: open a new Terminal window, type
   claude   and press Return, and follow the steps. (Without it, JARVIS still works.)

6. Face the camera. JARVIS greets you. Then just say "Hey" and its name (the one you picked,
   or "Jarvis") and what you want, in one breath: "Hey Jarvinky, what's the weather tomorrow?"

Talking to JARVIS
-----------------
  Say anything, the way you'd say it to a person. JARVIS works out what you mean.
    "Hey Jarvinky, open Spotify and turn the volume down to 30"
    "Hey Jarvinky, what's on my screen?"
    "Hey Jarvinky, run my Focus shortcut"
    "Hey Jarvinky, remember that my sister's birthday is June 3rd"
  If JARVIS asks you something, just answer: no need to say its name again.
  To interrupt it, just talk over it: it stops and listens to what you say instead
  ("stop", or a new request). With headphones a few words is enough; on the Mac's speakers,
  speak up, or say "Hey Jarvis" (that always works, and so does Option+Space).
    "Hey Jarvinky, what's on my calendar tomorrow?"
    "Hey Jarvinky, remind me to call Mum at 6"
    "Hey Jarvinky, text Sam that I'm running late"   (it reads it back; say yes to send)
  Option+Space also starts listening (allow it in System Settings > Privacy & Security >
  Accessibility). The menu bar icon has Talk, Start at login, and Quit.
  "Hey Jarvinky, goodbye" closes JARVIS.

To start again later, double-click "Start JARVIS.command".
To set it up for someone else, delete "user_settings.json" and start it again.
