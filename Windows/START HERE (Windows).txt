JARVIS - voice assistant for your Windows PC
=============================================

1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it (for example, Documents).

2. Double-click "Start JARVIS.bat".
   The first time, a window installs what JARVIS needs, including its speech recognition
   and natural voice (about 800 MB). This takes a few minutes and needs internet, but no
   admin password.

   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

3. A setup window appears. Type your name, pick a name for your assistant, choose its voice,
   then let it scan your face. That scan is the face lock: JARVIS starts only for you.

4. JARVIS asks once for a Claude API key in the black window. Paste one for the fastest
   answers (https://platform.claude.com/settings/keys, needs API credit), or press Enter to
   use Claude Code with your Claude account instead (https://claude.com/claude-code).

5. Face the camera. JARVIS greets you. Then just say "Hey" and its name (the one you picked,
   or "Jarvis") and what you want, in one breath: "Hey Jarvis, what's the weather tomorrow?"

Talking to JARVIS
-----------------
  Say anything, the way you'd say it to a person. Claude works out what you mean.
    "Hey Jarvis, open Spotify and turn the volume down to 30"
    "Hey Jarvis, what's on my screen?"
    "Hey Jarvis, remind me to call Mum at 6"            (it says the reminder out loud then)
    "Hey Jarvis, make a note: ideas for the trip..."     (saved in Documents\JARVIS Notes)
    "Hey Jarvis, write an email to sam@example.com..."   (opens a draft; you press Send)
    "Hey Jarvis, remember that my sister's birthday is June 3rd"
  If JARVIS asks you something, just answer: no need to say its name again.
  To interrupt it, just talk over it, or say "Hey Jarvis", or press Ctrl+Alt+J.
  JARVIS's icon sits in the system tray by the clock: Talk, Start when I sign in, Show log, Quit.
  "Hey Jarvis, goodbye" closes JARVIS.

Microphone
----------
  JARVIS listens on Windows' default microphone (Settings > System > Sound > Input). If that
  one hears nothing, it picks a microphone that does and says which in the window. To choose
  one yourself, add  "microphone": "part of its name"  to user_settings.json.

To start again later, double-click "Start JARVIS.bat".
To set it up for someone else, delete "user_settings.json" and start it again.
